A little help for freshers

Simple starting points for finding your feet at university.

🧭

Feeling lost?

Pick a known landmark, check the map, then follow the route link. If unsure, ask campus security or an official information desk.

🎓

Find academic spaces

Look up the library, administration block, halls and other learning spaces before your first class.

🛏️

Locate hostels

Explore hostel names listed for the main campus and check the precise building with campus staff.

🤝

Need a human guide?

Ask a student leader, official help desk, or campus security. Avoid following strangers to isolated places.
